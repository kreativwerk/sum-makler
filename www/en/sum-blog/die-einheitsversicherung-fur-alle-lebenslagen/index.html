One insurance policy for every situation in life?

Universal solution or myth? Why individual insurance is indispensable.

Insurance is like tailor-made clothing for your financial security. Yet one myth stubbornly persists: the idea of a kind of "one-size-fits-all insurance" that is supposed to be enough for every situation in life. Let's take a closer look and clear up this misunderstanding.

One policy fits all – why go tailor-made?

The myth of one-size-fits-all insurance is based on the idea that a general policy covers every eventuality. But life isn't standardised. Everyone has individual needs that vary depending on their life situation, job, marital status and financial circumstances.

The reality: a wide range of insurance needs

Let's say you're just starting out in your career. You need professional liability insurance to protect you against work-related risks. At this stage, however, a pension insurance policy is less relevant than occupational disability insurance. If you later start a family, children's insurance and adequate life insurance suddenly come into focus.

Real-life examples:

The young career starter:

Thomas is starting out as a software developer. He needs professional liability insurance to protect himself against work-related risks. An expensive endowment life insurance policy, on the other hand, would make less sense, as he doesn't have a family at this stage.

The young family:

Lisa and Tim have just got married and are expecting their first child. Now term life insurance is important to protect the partner and the child financially. Private supplementary health insurance can improve the family's healthcare.

The retiree:

Anita's children have left home and she is retiring. What she needs now is private long-term care insurance and suitable retirement provision to maintain her standard of living.

Conclusion: individual insurance solutions are essential

The idea of one-size-fits-all insurance may sound tempting, but it ignores the variety of individual needs. Tailor-made insurance gives you exactly the protection you need in your current stage of life. An experienced insurance expert can work with you to choose the right policies, making sure you're optimally covered – without paying for insurance you don't need. Don't settle for a one-size-fits-all policy; choose tailor-made cover that fits your life. After all, just as a suit or a dress is individually fitted, your insurance should also be tailored to your personal needs.
