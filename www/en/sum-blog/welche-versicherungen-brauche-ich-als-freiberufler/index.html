Which insurance do I need as a freelancer?

Tailor-made cover for freelancers: find out which insurance policies protect your self-employment.

As a freelancer, you're self-employed and responsible for your own business. Along the way, various risks can arise that could threaten your livelihood. To protect yourself against the financial consequences and run your business successfully, it's important to take out the right insurance. Here is a checklist of the recommended insurance policies for freelancers:

Insurance you should know about:

1. Professional liability insurance:

Professional liability insurance protects you against compensation claims by third parties that could arise from your professional work. It's particularly important if you could be held liable for mistakes or damage.

2. Business liability insurance:

Business liability insurance protects you against claims that could arise in the course of running your business, e.g. through client visits or production processes.

3. Occupational disability insurance:

As a freelancer, you depend on your ability to work. Occupational disability insurance gives you financial protection if you can no longer pursue your profession due to illness or an accident.

4. Health insurance:

As a freelancer, you need health insurance to receive medical care. Depending on your income, you can choose between statutory and private health insurance.

5. Legal expenses insurance:

Legal expenses insurance covers the costs of legal disputes and provides you with legal support if disagreements or conflicts arise.

6. Contents insurance:

Contents insurance protects your business equipment, such as office furniture, tools or computers, against damage caused by e.g. burglary, fire or water damage.

7. Cyber insurance:

Cyber insurance provides protection against the financial consequences of cyber attacks, data protection breaches or data loss that can affect your business.

8. Business contents insurance:

Business contents insurance protects your goods or stock against damage caused by theft, fire or other risks.

10. Pension insurance / retirement provision:

Plan ahead early for your financial future and take out a private pension insurance policy or another form of retirement provision so that you're well covered in retirement.

Business interruption insurance:

Business interruption insurance protects you against loss of income if your business temporarily comes to a standstill due to an insured loss.

This checklist gives you an overview of important insurance policies that are recommended for freelancers. Every business is unique, so it's advisable to discuss your individual insurance needs with an insurance adviser. That way, you can protect yourself comprehensively and run your business successfully.
