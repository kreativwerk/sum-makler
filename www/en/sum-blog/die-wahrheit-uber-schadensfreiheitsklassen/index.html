The truth about no-claims classes (Schadenfreiheitsklassen)

Clarity on no-claims classes: how they affect your car insurance.

No-claims classes (Schadenfreiheitsklassen) are a frequently misunderstood concept in car insurance. Many people think they mean you've never had a single accident. The truth, however, is more complex. No-claims classes are a system for rating your insurance history. The longer you drive without a claim, the higher you climb through the classes. A small claim doesn't necessarily mean losing your current class. It's important to know how claims affect your class and how the system actually works.

Myth: no-claims classes mean no accidents – is that true?

The assumption that no-claims classes mean you've never been involved in an accident isn't correct. Rather, no-claims classes reflect your insurance history. Even after a small accident, you won't necessarily lose your entire class and automatically pay more. It's important to understand the exact consequences.

The reality: no-claims classes as a rating system

Let's say you cause a minor parking dent. This doesn't necessarily mean you'll now slide down to the lowest class. There are often rules under which smaller claims aren't treated as serious blemishes on your insurance history. This can mean you stay in the same class and your premium doesn't rise significantly.

Real-life examples:

The minor parking bump:

You're parking and accidentally bump lightly into the car behind you. The repair costs are low, but you wonder whether this will affect your no-claims class. In most cases, it won't have a significant impact.

The collision with wildlife:

You collide with an animal on the road and your car is damaged. Since this is an unavoidable accident, it is generally not counted as a blemish on your insurance history.

Causing an accident:

You cause an accident that damages several cars. In this case your no-claims class could change, but it won't automatically drop back to the lowest level.

Conclusion: understand no-claims classes and assess them realistically

The truth about no-claims classes is less intimidating than the myth suggests. It's important to know how they work so that you aren't caught out by misunderstandings. A small accident doesn't necessarily mean losing your current class. Talk to an experienced insurance expert to get a clear picture of how claims could affect your no-claims class and what impact this could have on your premium.
