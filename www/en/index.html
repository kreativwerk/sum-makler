Easily confused…

An important distinction between an “insurance broker” and an “insurance agent”:
Here’s the difference:

vs

Broker

Legal basis: § 93 (1) sentence 1 HGB
(1) Anyone who, on a commercial basis and without being permanently engaged to do so under a contractual relationship, undertakes for other persons the brokering of contracts for the purchase or sale of goods or securities, insurance, the carriage of goods, ship chartering or other objects of commercial trade has the rights and obligations of a commercial broker. (Unofficial translation of the German Commercial Code.)
Acts for the client
Works on the customer's behalf
Unbound / independent
Insurer cannot give instructions
Free choice of products

Agent

Legal basis: § 84 (1) sentence 1 HGB
(1) A commercial agent is a self-employed trader who is permanently engaged to broker transactions for another business or to conclude them in its name. (Unofficial translation of the German Commercial Code.)
Acts for the insurer
Works on the insurer's behalf
Bound / dependent
Follows the insurer's instructions
Insurer's product range only
