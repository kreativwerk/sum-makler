Security for you and your furry friends

Protected together: security for you and your pets

Your pets are an important part of your family and deserve the best possible protection too. Liability insurance for pets offers you financial protection if your pet causes damage or injures people. It covers third-party compensation claims and protects you from financial burdens. In addition to liability insurance, you can also consider pet health insurance. It covers vet bills if your pet becomes ill or injured. With the right insurance cover, you ensure the safety of your furry friends and minimise the financial risks associated with them.

Frequently asked questions: Pet Owner Liability Insurance

What is pet owner liability insurance?
Pet owner liability insurance is insurance that covers damage caused by a pet. It protects the pet owner against third-party compensation claims that may arise, for example, from injuries or property damage.
Is pet owner liability insurance required by law?
In some countries or German federal states, pet owner liability insurance may be required by law, especially for certain pets such as dogs. It is important to check the relevant regulations of the country or region concerned.
Which damage is covered by pet owner liability insurance?
Pet owner liability insurance generally covers personal injury and property damage caused by the insured pet. This can include, for example, bite injuries, damage to other people's property or accidents.
Does pet owner liability insurance also apply abroad?
The territorial scope of pet owner liability insurance can vary. Some policies also offer cover abroad, while others may only be valid within your home country. It is important to check the policy terms to find out the exact scope of cover.
