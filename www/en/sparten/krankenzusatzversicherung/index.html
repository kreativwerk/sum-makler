More protection for your health: our supplementary insurance

Supplementary health insurance gives you additional protection for your health and complements your statutory health insurance. With supplementary health insurance, you can cover benefits such as treatment by the head physician, a single or double room in hospital, dentures and dental prosthetics, or alternative therapies. This kind of insurance gives you extended medical benefits and helps ensure you receive the best possible healthcare.

Frequently asked questions: Supplementary Health Insurance

What is supplementary health insurance?
Supplementary health insurance provides benefits in addition to statutory health insurance. It gives insured persons access to extended medical services, such as specialist treatment, free choice of hospital or treatment by the head physician.
Which benefits are covered by supplementary health insurance?
The exact benefits of supplementary health insurance vary depending on the policy. Typically, however, it covers benefits such as dentures and dental prosthetics, glasses and contact lenses, alternative therapies, better hospital accommodation, medicines, preventive check-ups and outpatient treatment.
Does supplementary health insurance make sense for everyone?
Whether supplementary health insurance makes sense depends on your individual needs and preferences. If you want additional medical benefits beyond the scope of statutory health insurance, supplementary health insurance can be a worthwhile addition.
Can I take out supplementary health insurance at any time?
As a rule, you can take out supplementary health insurance at any time. However, certain pre-existing conditions or current health problems may affect whether you can take out the policy or on what terms. It is advisable to check the conditions and health questions before signing the contract.
